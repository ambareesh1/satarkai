What SatarkAI is

A single scam-detection brain for the way India is actually targeted.

Scams no longer live in one inbox. They jump from a CBI-style phone call to a fake KYC SMS to a lottery WhatsApp to a refund email. SatarkAI watches all of those channels with the same engine, so the verdict is consistent — and explainable.

Satark सतर्क — stay alert

You give it the text. It extracts phones, UPI IDs, links, amounts, and OTP asks. It matches English and Hinglish scam signatures. Then it fuses that evidence into a SAFE / SUSPICIOUS / SCAM verdict with reasons you can read — not a black box.

The dashboard also includes a live phone simulator: tap Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, or Phone, open a sample, and scan it the way a real alert would fire.

Every channel it guards

If a scam can reach you here, SatarkAI can score it.

Each channel is only a thin adapter. The intelligence is shared — so a digital-arrest script is caught whether it arrives as a call transcript or a follow-up SMS.

Phone call

Digital-arrest and impersonation calls

Paste what the caller said — “CBI”, parcel with narcotics, stay on video, send money now. SatarkAI flags police impersonation, urgency, secrecy, and payment demands before fear takes over.

SMS

Fake KYC, account-block, and OTP theft

“Your SBI account will be BLOCKED today” plus a shady link is a classic. So is “share the OTP and CVV to stop fraud.” SatarkAI calls out the pressure, the credential grab, and the risky URL.

WhatsApp

Lottery forwards and prize traps

KBC lucky draws, registration fees, “send bank details.” Forward the chat into SatarkAI. Personal WhatsApp cannot be auto-read — the compliant model is paste or forward, then scan.

Notifications

Alerts that try to hijack your attention

Courier-customs fees, electricity disconnection, job-and-loan bait — they often land as banners first. SatarkAI reads the wording the same way, wherever you copied it from.

Email

Refund, tax, and phishing mail

Fake income-tax refunds and lookalike senders. Paste the subject and body — or scan a mailbox if you add an app password later. You still get the category, the entities, and the safe next step.

How it works

Three steps. A clear verdict. Advice you can follow.

1

Bring any message

Paste text, tap a sample in the phone simulator, or drop in a call transcript. Mark the channel so context stays honest.

2

The engine scores it

Patterns, entities, and sender/URL reputation fuse into 0–100. Optional LLM reasoning only runs if you add a key.

3

You get the why

SAFE, SUSPICIOUS, or SCAM — plus reasons, extracted UPI/phones/links, and plain-language advice. History stays in the dashboard.

What it is trained to notice

The scam scripts that keep showing up in India.

  • Impersonation

    Digital arrest / police–CBI

    Stay on the call, tell no one, pay to “close the case.”

  • Banking

    KYC and account-block

    Urgent links that steal logins before the “deadline.”

  • Credentials

    OTP, PIN, CVV theft

    Anyone asking for the code that just arrived is the fraud.

  • Prize bait

    Lottery and KBC forwards

    You never pay a fee to collect a prize you did not enter.

  • Money traps

    Job, loan, refund, crypto

    Instant money stories that end in a UPI to a stranger.

  • Fear scripts

    Courier, power, sextortion

    Customs fees, disconnection threats, and shame-based payouts.
